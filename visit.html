What to Expect

Your First Sunday

A Warm Welcome

Arrive anytime after 1:30 PM. Someone from our team will greet you at the door, answer your questions, and help you find a seat.

Worship & the Word

We open with heartfelt worship and then hear a practical, Bible-centered message from Pastor Matthew Hughes. You'll leave with something you can live out that week.

Real Family

Safe House is a new church, and that means you won't be lost in a crowd. Stay after service to meet people and hear how you can get connected.

Questions

Good to Know

What should I wear?

Whatever you're comfortable in. Some people dress up and some come casual. Come as you are.

Can I bring my kids?

Absolutely. Families are welcome at Safe House. Send us a note on the form above and we'll let you know what to expect for your kids.

Can I watch online first?

Yes. Every Sunday message is on our YouTube channel, and Wednesday Bible Study streams live on Facebook and YouTube.

I have a question that isn't here.

Call or text 602-338-3613 or email Connect@safehousechurch.org. We'd love to hear from you.
